CodeCup 2027 Tumbleweed: finalists ranked by points from white wins

Each program plays every final-round opponent twice, once as red and once as white. Red wins almost every game at the top of the table, so the white games are where programs are told apart: this table ranks the finalists by the points they scored in the final-round games they won as white, next to the official ranking from the same results file.
